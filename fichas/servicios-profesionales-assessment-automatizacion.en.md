## 1. Context and Challenge

The organization operates in a process-intensive environment covering regulatory compliance, quality management systems and training. Its broad customer base and the repetitive, scalable nature of many activities create significant potential for automation. At the same time, heterogeneous systems with limited integration and a high volume of manual tasks hinder operational efficiency, scalability and time-to-market improvements. The challenge is to identify genuine automation and artificial intelligence opportunities, assess their technical and functional feasibility, and establish a prioritised roadmap towards a more efficient operating model.

## 2. Technical Solution

Logicalis proposes a structured assessment covering three phases: AS-IS analysis, definition of the TO-BE target state and development of an implementation roadmap. The assessment will address three domains: AI-enabled process automation, technical task automation and user task automation through RPA. Use cases will be identified and prioritised across document processing, information classification, assisted content generation, internal assistants, system provisioning, configuration management, deployments, operations and administrative workflows. The reference architecture will include generative AI, AI Agents, Ansible and Terraform, complemented by RPA or low-code/no-code platforms to be selected during the assessment. Deliverables will include a process map, use-case catalogue, target architecture, recommended technology stack, dependencies, operating model and evolution plan. The assessment will also consider the applicable data protection and compliance requirements for the information processed.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

As this is an assessment proposal that has not yet been executed, the expected outcomes are focused on accelerating decision-making and preparing future implementations. The work will provide a comprehensive view of automation opportunities, a use-case catalogue prioritised according to feasibility and potential impact, and the identification of quick wins. It is also expected to reduce the technical uncertainty of subsequent initiatives through a target architecture and a case-by-case implementation strategy. The roadmap will support the planning of phases, effort, dependencies and evolution, promoting greater operational efficiency, scalable automation and increased autonomy for the internal team to develop new use cases.
