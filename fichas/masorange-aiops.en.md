## 1. Context and Challenge

The customer managed a global network and security infrastructure through a high-volume, predominantly reactive monitoring environment. Operations teams had to correlate alerts from different systems, prioritise incidents, consult technical documentation, and coordinate actions between the Service Desk and specialised support teams. This fragmentation made it difficult to establish the full context of each event, slowed resolution, and limited the consistent application of preventive or automated responses.

## 2. Technical Solution

Logicalis deployed LEIA (Logicalis Expert Intelligent Agentic), an agentic AIOps platform based on transversal AI, a central orchestrator, and specialised agents. The solution integrates observability signals, automation workflows, technical documentation, and incident management systems—including Zabbix, FortiManager, and ServiceNow—into a unified operating model. The orchestrator analyses the overall state of the systems, correlates events, interprets context, consults available knowledge, and determines whether to recommend an action, launch a predefined automation, or escalate the case to a specialised team. The architecture supports preventive and reactive use cases across network infrastructure, security, Windows systems, corporate directory services, virtualisation, and configuration management. The operating model incorporates security controls, decision traceability, human oversight for sensitive actions, and alignment with artificial intelligence governance, cybersecurity, and data protection frameworks and regulations, including the AI Act, ISO 42001, DORA, NIS2, CER, ENS, NIST, and GDPR, where applicable.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

The solution transformed the operating model into a more automated, efficient, and data-driven service. Contextual alert correlation improved incident triage and prioritisation, reducing reliance on manual tasks and enabling faster, more guided responses. Operations teams gained an integrated view of service status and could reuse technical knowledge and automated procedures from a common point. The platform also established a scalable foundation for adding new agents, observability sources, and auto-remediation use cases while maintaining the oversight and governance required to operate critical environments.
