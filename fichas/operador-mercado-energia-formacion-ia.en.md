## 1. Context and Challenge

The organisation sought to equip its executive team with the practical, secure and responsible use of generative artificial intelligence in the workplace. The challenge was to apply Microsoft Copilot capabilities to everyday activities such as preparing reports, committee materials, presentations, meetings, emails and data analyses, while maintaining verification practices, information protection and alignment with corporate cybersecurity policies. The proposal covered two training pathways: one focused on integration with Microsoft 365 applications and Copilot Studio, and another focused exclusively on Copilot Chat.

## 2. Technical Solution

A practical, participatory, in-person training programme was designed, covering generative AI fundamentals, secure usage, professional prompting, quality control and application to executive tasks. The advanced pathway included Microsoft Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, as well as the guided creation of an MVP agent in Copilot Studio, covering objectives, intents, sources, fallback mechanisms, a testing plan and operational metrics. The Copilot Chat pathway addressed the generation of drafts, summaries, templates and analysis proposals for subsequent use in corporate tools. The solution included guided exercises, prompt templates, validation checklists, meeting and committee kits, acceptance criteria, and recommendations for protecting, minimising and anonymising information.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

As this is a training offering that has not yet been delivered, the results are expressed as expected objectives. The training is expected to increase executives’ autonomy in using generative AI, accelerate the preparation of reports, presentations, meetings and decision-making materials, and encourage more consistent adoption of these capabilities. It is also expected to establish a common foundation for safe and responsible usage, support the identification and prioritisation of use cases, and prepare the subsequent deployment of AI across other areas of the organisation. In the Copilot Studio pathway, building an MVP agent and defining a testing plan and metrics should provide a practical reference for future automation initiatives.
