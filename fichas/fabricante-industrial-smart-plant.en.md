## 1. Context and Challenge

An industrial organization seeks to comprehensively monitor the condition of multiple manufacturing plants and move towards a more proactive operating model. The challenge stems from the lack of continuous monitoring at certain facilities and the occurrence of unplanned downtime, particularly at high-demand plants. The proposal aims to standardize analytical models and digital solutions across different plant types, starting with facilities located in Chile.

## 2. Technical Solution

Smart Plant is conceived as an AI-based virtual supervision platform, featuring interactive and customized dashboards to visualize the condition of the factory. The solution would incorporate real-time monitoring, anomaly detection, smart alarms, and an ecosystem of autonomous agents capable of triggering notifications, automated responses, and operational processes. It also includes an enquiry chatbot and the generation of daily reports. Future functional development would integrate inventory management and maintenance planning through M3, as well as the automatic generation of purchase requisitions in Oracle. Deployment would be organized in phases across several plants in Chile, with an estimated duration of ten months, followed by an evolution phase.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

As this is a proposal in the planning phase, the results are expected and subject to project execution. Smart Plant would improve operational visibility across the plants, anticipate anomalies, and reduce the impact of unplanned downtime through faster and more automated responses. It is also expected to facilitate the standardization of analysis across facilities, accelerate the preparation of daily reports, and connect industrial supervision with inventory, maintenance, and procurement processes. The proposed architecture should support scalability across different plant types and increase the autonomy of operational teams through dashboards, a chatbot, and intelligent agents.
