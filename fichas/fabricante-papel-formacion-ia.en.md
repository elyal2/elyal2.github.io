## 1. Context and Challenge

An industrial organisation with sensitive data seeks to accelerate the practical adoption of artificial intelligence without creating technology overload or security risks. The proposal addresses the need to transform a set of isolated courses into a progressive capability journey, differentiating the needs of technical teams from those of business profiles. The challenge includes preparing teams to develop generative AI solutions, integrate agents with corporate systems, and establish criteria for data sovereignty, prompt privacy, regulatory compliance, cost control and observability.

## 2. Technical Solution

A six-week training programme is proposed, structured around learning sprints: Foundation, Enablement and Acceleration, followed by an Amplification phase to extend capabilities across the organisation. The technical track covers Azure and AWS architectures, AI models, advanced prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation over corporate data, agent development, API and Model Context Protocol integration, and the use of assisted development environments. In parallel, the business track focuses on Copilot Studio, Power Automate, no-code automation and the identification of relevant use cases. The programme starts with a joint responsible and secure AI session covering GDPR, the EU AI Act, data sovereignty, personal information, corporate secrets, guardrails, auditing, approvals and consumption optimisation. The programme concludes with a joint hackathon involving IT and business champions to build functional prototypes, supported by technical documentation, best-practice guides, recordings and an evolution roadmap.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

As this is a training proposal that has not yet been executed, the expected results depend on team participation, environment availability and programme continuity. The journey is expected to progressively increase the technical staff’s autonomy in designing and deploying AI solutions, while improving the business’s ability to identify opportunities and create automations with immediate impact. It should also accelerate the creation of functional micro-PoCs through the hackathon, reduce the time required to move from an idea to a prototype, and improve secure adoption through a common framework for governance, compliance and cost control. The documentation and materials delivered will support self-service, the replication of use cases and the subsequent scaling of AI capabilities.
