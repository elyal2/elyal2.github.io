## 1. Context and Challenge

An organization in the audiovisual media sector needed to strengthen the operation and evolution of its Big Data and Analytics environment. The scope covered cloud platform management, new developments, evolutionary and corrective maintenance, and technical support for business teams. The challenge included ensuring data service availability, responding to critical incidents outside business hours, and maintaining the flexibility required to address new analytical and technology requirements. The proposed approach involved integrating a specialised team into the client's operating model, working in coordination with its internal operations, development, and monitoring teams.

## 2. Technical Solution

The solution consisted of a remote managed service delivered by a mixed team of four dedicated profiles: a senior DevOps/SysOps specialist, a senior data engineer specialised in AWS and Snowflake, and two junior data engineers focused on AWS. The service covered AWS platform administration and scalability, data component maintenance, identity and access management, monitoring, deployments, and operational continuity. The technology ecosystem included AWS, Amazon S3, Amazon Athena, Amazon DynamoDB, AWS Glue, AWS Lambda, AWS Step Functions, Amazon SageMaker, AWS CloudFormation, CloudWatch, Snowflake, Power BI, Python, Apache Spark, Terraform, and Jenkins. Development and maintenance activities were organised using Scrum, with Jira for managing requests, incidents, and the backlog, and with progressive releases across development, testing, and production environments. The operating model included preparation, transition, shadowing, regular operations, and service handback phases, together with on-demand access to specialists and architects for technical escalations. Remote operations used secure VPN or equivalent access mechanisms, identity and permissions management, service monitoring, backups, and procedures aligned with IT service management and ITIL best practices.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

As this was a proposed managed service that had not yet been implemented when the documentation was prepared, the outcomes were expressed as expected benefits. The service was expected to improve the continuity and responsiveness of the data environment through expanded on-call coverage in a 24x7x365 model and a structured approach to critical incident management. The addition of specialised profiles was expected to accelerate the delivery of new developments, evolutionary maintenance, and incident resolution, while strengthening the capacity for ad hoc analysis and reporting for the business. The service was also intended to support the scalability of the AWS and Snowflake platforms, optimise the operation of cloud components, and provide greater predictability through service-level agreements, transition processes, and replacement mechanisms. Collaboration with the internal team and access to external specialists were expected to promote knowledge transfer, technical autonomy, and the continuous evolution of the Analytics platform.
