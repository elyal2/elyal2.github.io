## 1. Context and Challenge

Context and Challenge

The organization needs to rapidly and systematically assess the evolution of its Power BI environment towards Microsoft Fabric. The challenge is to understand the current state of datasets, data sources, volumes, users, consumption patterns and licensing, while also identifying the technical implications of a potential migration. The assessment places particular focus on information consumed through Power Apps and Power Automate, including the limitations of access interfaces and data exposure mechanisms. The proposal covers a focused evaluation of one or two representative datasets, supported by a short technical consulting engagement.

## 2. Technical Solution

Technical Solution

The assessment will be structured around three workstreams: reviewing the existing Power BI environment, evaluating the technical feasibility of migrating to Fabric, and sizing the capacity strategy for development and production. Architecture alternatives based on Fabric components, such as Lakehouse and other storage and modelling options, will be analysed together with the compatibility of consumption patterns with Direct Lake. The impact on Power Apps, access restrictions through APIs and XMLA, and the available options for exposing data to applications will also be assessed. The outcome will be an executive report containing the recommended short- and medium-term target architecture, indicative capacity sizing, and a licensing rationalization strategy.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

Business Impact and Results

As this is a proposed assessment that has not yet been executed, the expected outcomes are focused on reducing technical uncertainty and supporting an informed migration decision. The report will provide a consolidated view of the current environment, identify limitations and dependencies, compare architecture alternatives, and establish a capacity recommendation for development and production. It is also expected to improve the predictability of Fabric consumption, clarify the impact on Power Apps and Power Automate, and provide an initial roadmap for a scalable and sustainable evolution of the analytics environment.
