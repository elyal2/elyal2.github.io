## 1. Context and Challenge

The public authority wanted to understand how artificial intelligence can transform day-to-day work and improve efficiency, creativity and collaboration across its teams. The proposal addressed the need to provide accessible training for people from different departments and with varying levels of technical expertise, with particular attention to finance, customer service and legal operations. The challenge was to translate the potential of Microsoft Copilot into concrete workplace scenarios while maintaining a responsible perspective on privacy, security, errors and potential bias.

## 2. Technical Solution

A practical and collaborative workshop focused on Microsoft Copilot and its integration with the Microsoft 365 and Azure environments was proposed. The session would combine an introduction to artificial intelligence, interactive demonstrations and group exercises covering tasks such as summarising emails and meetings, creating documents and presentations, analysing data in Excel and generating actionable insights. It would also cover best practices for structuring prompts, functional scenarios for finance, customer service and legal teams, as well as advanced capabilities for integration with external systems and Copilot customisation. The training would include responsible AI principles, privacy, security, human review of results and resources for continued learning.

## 3. Business Impact and Results

As this was a training proposal that had not yet been delivered, the expected outcomes were focused on accelerating the safe adoption of AI in the workplace. The workshop could help reduce the time spent on repetitive tasks, improve the preparation of reports and presentations, and support more effective collaboration between departments. It would also enable participants to gain greater autonomy in applying Copilot to their workflows, identify automation opportunities and assess its outputs according to security, quality and responsibility criteria.
